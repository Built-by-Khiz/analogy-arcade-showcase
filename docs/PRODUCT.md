# Product brief

## Problem

A technically correct explanation can still leave someone confused. Unfamiliar jargon and examples make a new topic harder to connect with.

## Product promise

Explain an idea through a world the learner already knows, then make the analogy’s limits explicit. Analogy Arcade is designed for curious learners, technical beginners, product builders, and busy decision makers.

## Core journey

The learner chooses a topic and interest world, selects an audience, and chooses output formats. The app presents a learning card that can include a simple explanation, analogy, concept mapping, limitations, and an interactive quiz. Feedback captures usefulness and what needs improvement.

## Intentional choices

- Custom interests keep personalization open-ended.
- Suggested presets reduce the effort of getting started.
- Audience controls change the level of explanation.
- Optional shorter or funnier output helps the card fit the learner.
- A sample card demonstrates value without consuming AI quota.
- The quiz hides its answer until the learner chooses an option.
- The interface shows input summaries so the learner can see what shaped the card.

## Boundaries

The product is an educational demo, not a substitute for professional advice. It asks users to avoid private or sensitive inputs. The daily demo allowance is shared and protects the service’s limited AI capacity.

## What success would look like

Useful measures include successful completed explanations, helpfulness ratings, quiz engagement, feedback themes, and whether users find the selected analogy relevant. These are evaluation ideas, not reported performance claims.
